# 📡 World 2: The Digital Broadcast

**Goal:** Move the activity out of the local environment and make it accessible to students. You will choose your preferred ecosystem to host the `.html` file you created in World 1.

**The Setting:** A bustling, neon-lit communications tower resembling an 80s-style radio station switchboard. 

**Your Guide:** The Signal Master (An energetic operator wearing a retro headset, frantically patching glowing fiber-optic cables).

---

## 🏗️ Quest 4: The Preparation (Platform Selection)

*The Signal Master says: "A localized artifact is safe, Vibe-Coder, but it helps no one if it's trapped on your hard drive! It is time to broadcast. Review your district’s infrastructure and choose your frequency. Will you transmit through the Google array, the SharePoint network, or the open-source GitHub channels?"*

**Action:** 
Evaluate and select your hosting platform. Set up your blank broadcasting environment using one of these three paths:

*   **Google Sites Route:** Navigate to `sites.google.com`. Click the "+" to create a new blank site. Name your site (e.g., "Mr. Smith's Interactive Arcade") and clear the header space.
*   **SharePoint Route:** Log into your Microsoft 365 dashboard and open SharePoint. Create a new "Communication Site" or navigate to an existing class site. Create a new blank Page.
*   **GitHub Pages Route:** Go to `github.com` and log in or create a free account. Click "New Repository," name it (e.g., "class-arcade"), set it to "Public," check the box to add a README file, and click "Create repository."

---

## 🚀 Quest 5: The Deployment (Hosting)

*The Signal Master says: "The channel is open! Lock the code into the embedding matrix. Copy the payload exactly as the Canvas provided it, and patch it directly into the mainframe!"*

**Action:** 
Deploy the raw `.html` code from World 1 into your chosen platform:

*   **Google Sites Route:** 
    1. On your blank site, double-click the empty space and select the yellow `< > Embed` icon.
    2. Click the **"Embed code"** tab.
    3. Paste your entire HTML/CSS/JS code block and click "Next," then "Insert." 
    4. Drag the blue handles to resize the widget so the entire activity is visible.
*   **SharePoint Route:** 
    1. On your blank page, click the "+" icon to add a new web part.
    2. Search for and select the **"Embed"** web part.
    3. Paste your raw code directly into the "Website address or embed code" field. *(Note: If your district blocks raw code embeds in SharePoint, upload your `.html` file directly to your site's Document Library, copy its sharing link, and use the "File Viewer" web part to display it).*
*   **GitHub Pages Route:** 
    1. Inside your new repository, click "Add file" > "Upload files."
    2. Upload the `.html` file you saved to your desktop in Quest 3. Click "Commit changes."
    3. Navigate to the repository's **Settings** tab. Click **Pages** in the left sidebar.
    4. Under "Build and deployment" > "Branch," change "None" to "main" (or "master") and click "Save." GitHub will generate a live URL for your file within a few minutes.

## 📱 Quest 6: The Quality Assurance (Testing & Sharing)

*The Signal Master says: "Signal is pulsing, but we need to verify the integrity on the receiving end. Grab your mobile scanner! If the layout shatters on a small screen, the broadcast is a failure. Check the feed, then send me the final coordinates to complete the uplink!"*

**Action:** 
1.  **Publish:** Click the final "Publish" or "Post" button on your chosen platform to push the site live to the web.
2.  **Mobile Scan:** Open the published URL on your smartphone or tablet. Verify that the activity is fully playable, the text is readable, and the buttons are easily tappable on a smaller screen. (If it is broken, return to the Canvas with an "Iteration Prompt" from Quest 2 to fix the responsive layout, then replace the code).
3.  **Submit Coordinates:** Copy the public, live URL of your published game for submission.
